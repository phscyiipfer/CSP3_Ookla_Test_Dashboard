❓ CSP3 Ookla Dashboard – Help & Guide

The dashboard reads the shared Google Sheet of speed-test results and refreshes itself. Everything below the header reacts to your thresholds and filters.

  1. Check the sync status under the title: Live & Synced means the data is current. Data refreshes every 30 seconds while Auto Refresh is ON; press Refresh Data to refresh right now.
  2. Set the thresholds in Threshold Settings (Mbps) for 4G and 5G download and upload. Every status, count, chart and pin updates as soon as you change a value. Reset Limits restores the defaults (4G 5/1, 5G 25/3). Limits are not saved when you reload the page.
  3. Read the Main Test Summary: total logs, valid samples, 4G and 5G pass rates, and the average download, upload and latency of valid samples.
  4. Read the Failure Count Breakdown: for 4G and 5G, how many failed samples missed only the download limit, only the upload limit, or both. Each failed sample is counted once.
  5. Filter the data with date, start/end hour, status, testers, servers, cities, technologies and keyword search. In the multi-select lists, Ctrl/Cmd + click picks several items. Deselect All Multi-Selects clears the lists; Clear Filters resets everything. All summary numbers, charts, the map and the table show only the filtered samples.
  6. Use the charts: they show the latest 15 filtered tests (throughput, latency and signal strength).
  7. Explore the map: choose Map, Satellite or Dark at the top-right. The round crosshair button (top-left) shows your own location. Click a pin for details, or click a row in the log table to jump to its pin.
  8. Import your own map layers: press 📂 Import layers above the map (or drop files on the map) to add a CSV / tab-delimited file with lat and lon columns, a Shapefile (.zip, or the .shp, .dbf and .prj files selected together), a GeoPackage (.gpkg) or a GeoJSON file. Each layer gets a chip with a show/hide tick, a colour picker, a zoom button and a remove button. Click a feature to see its attributes. Layers last until you reload the page.
  9. Add remarks in the last table column: press Edit, type, then Save. The remark is written back to the Google Sheet.
  10. Export with Export CSV or Export Excel. The file contains only the filtered rows, with every column of the sheet. On iPhone/iPad the share sheet opens so you can choose Save to Files.
  11. Hide what you don't need: every section has a Hide / Show button beside its title, and the header has Collapse all / Expand all. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
Live data
  • Reads the Google Sheet through its Apps Script link; auto refresh every 30 s (can be switched off) and manual refresh.
  • Times are shown in Philippine time (PHT, UTC+8).
Thresholds
  • Editable 4G and 5G download/upload limits that drive every status and count.
Main Test Summary
  • Total logs (filtered, with the overall total), valid samples, 5G and 4G pass rate, average download, upload and latency.
Failure Count Breakdown
  • Six counters: 4G and 5G each split into download only, upload only, and download and upload both. Failed or empty tests count as both.
Filters
  • Date, start hour, end hour, status, testers, server names, city/location, technology and keyword search; lists keep your selection through refreshes.
Charts
  • Throughput (download on the left axis, upload on the right), latency, and signal strength (RSRP) for the latest 15 filtered tests.
Map
  • Google map tiles with Map, Satellite and Dark views; green, amber and red pins for pass, below threshold and failed; a status legend; a Find my location button; pins open a detail popup.
  • Import extra layers (CSV / tab-delimited, Shapefile, GeoPackage, GeoJSON) with colour, show/hide, zoom and remove controls, and attribute popups.
Test log table
  • All filtered tests with status badges; click a row to focus its map pin; editable remarks saved to the sheet.
Export
  • CSV and Excel (.xlsx) of the filtered rows with all sheet fields plus Status Category and Time (PHT); iPhone/iPad share-sheet support.
Layout and look
  • Hide/Show for every section and Collapse all / Expand all (remembered); dark theme with the opti / CSP3 branding and pattern background; works on desktop and phone.
How a test is classified
  • FAILED / NULL: no usable result (download or upload missing or zero) or the sheet status says failed. Excluded from valid samples and averages.
  • BELOW THRESHOLD: a valid result where download or upload is under the limit for its technology. Counted as a valid sample.
  • PASS: a valid result that meets both limits.
  • Technology: any entry containing "5G" is 5G; entries containing "4G" or "LTE" are 4G.
Failure breakdown rule
  • Only the download missed: counted under DL Failure. Only the upload missed: UL Failure. Both missed (or nothing measured): counted only under DL & UL Failed.
Troubleshooting
  • Sync Failed: check your internet connection and that the Apps Script link is still deployed, then press Refresh Data.
  • Export failed – Apps Script does not return all sheet fields: the Apps Script needs the full-sheet option (?view=full).
  • Export failed – sheet changed: press Refresh Data, then export again.
  • Map shows grey squares: the map tiles could not load; check your connection and refresh the page.
  • Find my location does nothing: allow location access for this page in the browser (it needs a secure page, and some embedded views block it).
  • Layer import fails: CSV needs lat/lon (or latitude/longitude) columns in degrees, or a WKT/geometry column. A Shapefile needs its .prj (use a .zip, or select .shp + .dbf + .prj together). MapInfo TAB/MAP files cannot be read in a browser: export them to GeoPackage, Shapefile or CSV first. Shapefile and GeoPackage import need an internet connection to load a helper library.
  • Remark did not save: the button shows Retry; check your connection and try again.
  • Numbers look low: check the filters; they apply to every number, chart and the map, even when the filter section is hidden.
